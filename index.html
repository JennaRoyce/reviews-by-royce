Welcome to Reviews by Royce!

Hi, I’m Jenna, and welcome to my little corner of the book world. I am an avid reader with a soft spot for fantasy and romance, especially stories with strong female leads, immersive world building, and the kind of happy ending that makes closing the book feel worth it.

On this site you will find brief summaries, thoughtful reviews, and spice-scale ratings. My hope is to create a space where readers can find helpful opinions, revisit the magic of a world they just left, and maybe even discover their next obsession.

Whether you are searching for a new favorite or just want to relive a book you cannot stop thinking about, I hope you find something here to love.
